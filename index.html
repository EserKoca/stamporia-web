Stamporia

An elegant home for your stamp collection, with a studio for designing new stamps.

  • Smart Scan reads the country, year and face value from a photo of your stamp, entirely on your device.
  • Albums by country, theme or series, with empty slots for the stamps you are missing.
  • Value tracking, a wishlist and a world map of your collection.
  • Your collection syncs through your own iCloud account.
  • Stamp Studio turns any photo into a perforated postage stamp.
