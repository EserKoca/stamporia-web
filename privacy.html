Stamporia - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Overview

Stamporia is a stamp collection and stamp design app developed by A.E.K. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Handle

Stamporia handles only the data needed to provide its features:

  • Photos: Used to catalogue your stamps and to design new stamps. Smart Scan, which crops a stamp photo and reads text such as the country, year and face value, runs entirely on your device. Your photos are never sent to us.
  • Collection data: The stamp details, albums and notes you enter.
  • Purchase history: In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed and managed by Apple. The app checks them on your device to unlock Premium features.

Data Storage and iCloud Sync

Your collection is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your collection, including stamp photos, is also stored in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) so it stays in sync across your devices. This data is kept by Apple in your iCloud account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We, the developer, cannot access it.

You can stop syncing at any time by turning off iCloud for Stamporia in your device's Settings. We do not operate any servers of our own and do not collect your personal data.

Third-Party Services

Stamporia uses only Apple services: StoreKit for purchases and iCloud (CloudKit) for sync. We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Camera and Photo Library Access

Stamporia uses the camera to photograph stamps and to create new stamp designs. When you choose a photo from your library, only the photos you select are shared with the app. Stamporia asks for permission to add photos to your library only when you save a stamp you designed. You can manage these permissions in your device Settings.

Children's Privacy

Stamporia does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties. When you use the share feature, you control what is shared and with whom through the standard iOS share sheet.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
