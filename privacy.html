Stamporia - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Overview

Stamporia is a photo stamp creation app developed by A.E.K. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

Stamporia collects minimal data to provide its core functionality:

  • Photos and Videos: Used solely to create stamps within the app. Photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.
  • Purchase History: In-app purchase records are managed by Apple and stored on your device to unlock premium features.

Data Storage

All your data, including stamps, preferences, and photos, is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers and do not collect or transmit any personal data.

Third-Party Services

Stamporia uses Apple's StoreKit framework for in-app purchases. These transactions are handled entirely by Apple according to their privacy policy. We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Camera and Photo Library Access

Stamporia requests access to your camera and photo library solely for the purpose of creating stamps. Camera access is used to take new photos, and photo library access is used to select existing photos and save created stamps. These permissions can be managed in your device Settings.

Children's Privacy

Stamporia does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties. When you use the share feature, you control what is shared and with whom through the standard iOS share sheet.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
